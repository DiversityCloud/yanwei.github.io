<p style="text-align: justify;">
  I am currently a University-appointed Associate Professor at the School of Computer and Information (School of Artificial Intelligence), Anhui Normal University. I received my Ph.D. from the College of Intelligence and Computing at Tianjin University in June 2024. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the School of EECS, Peking University, where I worked with <a href="https://cuibinpku.github.io/papers.html">Prof. Bin Cui</a> at the <a href="https://github.com/PKU-DAIR">PKU-DAIR Lab</a>. My research interests include online social networks, recommender systems, and machine learning.
</p>
